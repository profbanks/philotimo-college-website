Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Applicants usually need clarity on approval, course choice, entry requirements, and the enrollment process.

Are the listed departments NBTE-recognised programme titles?

The department list uses NBTE public programme nomenclature for planning and presentation. Institutional accreditation status should be confirmed with the school and NBTE before admission publicity is finalised.

Can I apply while waiting for O'Level results?

Yes. The form allows enquiry and preliminary application. Final admission depends on the department's screening requirements and complete credentials.

Does the site support online course enrollment?

Yes. Applicants can pick a course, submit details, generate a reference number, and have the application reviewed through the administrator desk.

Can the portal connect to payment and student records?

The local backend now supports application records, status checks, administrator review, and enrolment. A payment gateway, SMS, and email workflow can be integrated when the live production backend is selected.
